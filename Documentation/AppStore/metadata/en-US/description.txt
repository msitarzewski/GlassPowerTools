Terminals, databases, remote desktops and an editor for every machine you work on, in one app built for Apple Vision Pro, iPad and iPhone.

Add a machine once. Its address lives on the host, and each way in is a service: a terminal over SSH, a database, a remote desktop. Connect opens the one you choose, or all of them. Change the address, and every service follows.

VISION PRO FIRST
Each session is a window you place around you, so a server's terminal, its database and its desktop can sit side by side in your room. Every window is glass: Opacity sets how much colour is painted, and Glass how much the room is frosted behind it. A window you aren't using can shrink into a small glass orb, with its session live inside, until you pinch it back. A database's tables and keys can stand in the room as a diagram in three dimensions.

TERMINAL
• SSH with host keys you confirm, and a stop if a key ever changes
• Password and key sign-in, including keys made in the Secure Enclave
• Tabs, a grid or a carousel of terminals, themes and workgroups
• Port forwarding and an SFTP file browser
• Keep Sessions Running with tmux: a dropped connection, a closed window or a quit app leaves the work running, and Active picks the session up on another device

DATABASES
• MySQL, PostgreSQL and SQLite
• Browse, filter and edit rows, follow foreign keys, write and explain queries
• A live diagram of every table and key
• Connect directly with TLS, through the machine's SSH connection, or through another machine
• Mark a database read-only
• Ask the on-device assistant for a query, on devices with Apple Intelligence

REMOTE DESKTOPS
• RDP and VNC, including the Mac's own Screen Sharing, encrypted once you sign in with a Mac account
• The remote resolution follows your window
• Image quality that adapts to the connection, audio, and a shared clipboard

EDITOR
• Files on this device or on a server, and projects with a file tree
• Syntax highlighting for fifteen languages
• Previews for Markdown (with Mermaid diagrams), HTML, SVG and CSV
• Pictures, PDFs, video, audio and 3D models open as themselves

EVERYWHERE YOU ARE
• Your library, favorites, workgroups and projects sync through iCloud, and passwords and keys through iCloud Keychain. Keys made in the Secure Enclave never leave their device.
• Active lists every open session on every device
• Widgets, Siri and Shortcuts, Spotlight and Handoff, and Controls on iPhone and iPad

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no analytics, no tracking. The app connects to the machines you add and to iCloud, and to Tailscale only if you connect a Tailscale account to find your devices. No server of ours sits between you and your machines.
