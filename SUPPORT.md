# Support

Glass Power Tools is free and made by one person. Here is where to find answers and how to ask.

## Find an answer

The [help](https://glasspowertools.com/help/) covers each part of the app on each device: the
library, the terminal, databases, remote desktops and the editor.

## Ask a question or report a problem

Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/msitarzewski/GlassPowerTools/issues). Search the open
issues first, in case someone has asked already. Please include:

- the app's version (**Settings ▸ About**) and your device;
- what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.

Never include passwords, keys, or the real names and addresses of your machines.

If you would rather not use GitHub, write to <msitarzewski@gmail.com>.

## Security problems

Please report these privately, not in a public issue, as described in [Security](SECURITY.md).

## Ideas and contributions

Ideas are welcome as issues. To contribute code, see [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Privacy

What the app keeps and sends is in [Privacy](PRIVACY.md).
