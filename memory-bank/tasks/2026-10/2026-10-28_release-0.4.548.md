## The Files window's gap (cause)
A Mac window opened on an empty folder: the file list fills its window, but `ContentUnavailableView` is only as tall
as its words, so the window's first content was small and centred, and nothing painted around it (the window is
clear glass; `GlassLayeredWindow`'s paint is behind the content). Measured on his Mac by accessibility: the window
911 pt tall, the content from 650 pt. Opening a folder with files first never showed it. Since 0.4.491 at least.
