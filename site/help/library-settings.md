# Settings and window appearance

Settings for the whole app are in one place. This page covers opening Settings, the **General**, **Appearance**, **Privacy** and **About** pages, and how to make windows more or less transparent. The pages after **About** (**Connections**, **Terminal**, **Snippets** and **Editor**) are described in the help for those features.

## Open Settings

- **On Vision Pro:** choose **Settings** (gear) in the bar below the library window. Settings opens in its own window, with its pages as tabs beside it.
- **On Mac:** choose **Glass Power Tools** ▸ **Settings…** (⌘,). The pages are icons across the top of the window, and Settings reopens on the page you last used.
- **On iPad:** choose **Settings** (gear) in the library's toolbar. Choose a page from the control at the top, and choose **Done** when you finish.
- **On iPhone:** choose **Settings** (gear) in the library's toolbar, then choose a page from the list. Every page has **Done**.

## Change how transparent windows are

Choose **Appearance**. The **Glass** section has:

- **Opacity**: how strongly the theme color is painted behind content, from transparent to opaque.
- **Frost**: how strongly the background behind a window is blurred, from clear to frosted. **On Mac:** the title bar follows this setting too.
- **Material**: the kind of frost: **Light**, **Medium**, **Heavy** or **Max**. It is unavailable while **Frost** is at 0%.
- **Reset Appearance**: returns to the starting settings: **Opacity** 0%, **Frost** 100% and **Material** **Heavy**.

The sliders settle on 0%, 50% and 100% when you drag near them. Text and controls stay fully visible at every setting. For a completely clear window, set **Opacity** and **Frost** to 0%.

**On Vision Pro:** windows use the system's own glass. **Opacity** paints the theme color over it; **Frost** and **Material** do not add a second layer to the library's windows.

These settings also apply to terminal windows. They are kept on each device.

> [!NOTE]
> When **Reduce Transparency** is on in the system's Accessibility settings, every window is opaque and frosted, and these controls are unavailable until you turn it off.

## General

**General** shows the state of your host library on this device under **Host Library**: how many **Hosts** it has and whether **This device** is kept on this device only. If the file that holds favorites and recent hosts cannot be read, an orange message here explains why, and favorites and **Recent** cannot be changed until it can.

## Privacy

**Credentials** shows where passwords and keys are kept and their **Default mobility**. With **iCloud Keychain**, a credential you save is available on your other devices. Nothing on this page shows or reads a secret.

**On your other devices** shows the **Host library** sync status and explains which secrets travel with you. See "Your library on all your devices".

## About

**About** shows the app's version, a **Sponsor** link and **Acknowledgements**.

Choose **Acknowledgements** to see every third-party component in the app, grouped by what it is used for. Search by name or licence, and choose a component to read its licence text and see where its source is published. Choose **Done** to close the list.
